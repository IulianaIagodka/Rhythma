## Description

Your cycle isn’t an interruption. It’s part of your rhythm.

Rhythma Cycle is a simple, private period tracker designed to help you understand your cycle and plan ahead—without clutter, pressure, or complicated charts.

### LOG YOUR PERIOD IN ONE TAP
Record the first day of your period immediately or choose another date.

### SEE YOUR WHOLE YEAR
Explore a clear annual calendar showing your recorded periods and predicted cycle days in one place.

### PLAN AHEAD
Get an estimated start date for your next period based on your cycle history.

### OPTIONAL OVULATION ESTIMATES
Choose whether you want estimated ovulation days displayed in your calendar.

### MADE FOR YOUR RHYTHM
See your current cycle day at a glance and use your cycle as helpful context when planning work, rest, exercise, travel, and important events.

### PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your cycle data is stored locally on your iPhone. No account is required.

### PERSONALISE YOUR EXPERIENCE
Choose between light, dark, and system appearance. Rhythma Cycle is available in English and Ukrainian.

Rhythma Cycle provides estimates only. Its predictions are not medical advice and should not be used as contraception.
